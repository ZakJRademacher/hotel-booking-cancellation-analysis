03THE NEXT CHECK

Treat Online TA and Groups as two separate leads.

For the City Hotel in 2016, Online TA produced 7,149 cancellations, the largest workload. Groups cancelled at 74.7%, the highest rate among segments with 100 or more bookings. Before trialling a confirmation process, compare lead times, deposit terms and arrival-month mix for each group.
